Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-15. In effect from 15 November 2026.

Espresso Martini is a free iPhone app made and run by Magnus Stjernström, an individual. He is responsible for the data described here. “We” and “us” mean him and the people he asks to help moderate the app.

In short: everything you post is public. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no data is sold or shared for advertising. Our server never stores your position. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Everything you post is public

Anyone who uses the app can see these, including people who are not signed in:

  • your username and your profile photo (a profile photo may show you);
  • your ratings: the bar, stars, price, tags, comment, photos, and when you posted and last edited them;
  • your ranked list of bars;
  • who you follow and who follows you, and how many cheers each rating has.

The app’s public data also holds a few things the app does not display: your account’s internal number (which ties your ratings, photos and list to you) and the date your account was created. Someone who reads the public data directly, rather than through the app, can see them.

Photos are stored as public files: anyone with a photo’s web address can open it, and that address begins with your internal account number. The app removes location and camera details from every photo before uploading it.

Blocking someone stops contact inside the app, not viewing: someone who signs out can still see what you post.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising or analytics code in the app.
  • Our server never stores your position. Your phone remembers the last map area you viewed (see below), and Apple receives areas around you for the bar suggestion and search (see Location).
  • No contacts, and no location or camera details from your photos.
  • No data is sold, and none is shared for advertising.

Kept only on your phone

Your sign-in session, a copy of your profile so the app opens quickly, whether the app has already asked about notifications, and the last map area you viewed (centred on you when the map had just centred on you).

Location

Location is optional and used only if you allow it.

  • The map sends our server the area you are looking at, to show the bars in it, never your coordinates as such. When the map opens or recentres on you, that area is centred on where you are, so the server can learn roughly where you are from it.
  • The Nearby feed sends our server a position rounded to about 7 miles (about 11 km), to find ratings within about 50 miles.
  • The bar suggestion, when you rate, asks Apple Maps for bars in a small area, a few hundred yards across, around where you are. Searching for a bar while rating asks Apple Maps about an area of a few miles around you. Map search asks Apple Maps about the area on screen.
  • Our server does not store the map area or the rounded position. Your phone remembers the last map area you viewed, so the map opens there next time; when the map had just centred on you, that area is centred on where you were.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app sends notifications through Apple’s push service when someone follows you or cheers your rating. A notification shows the other person’s username and, for a cheers, the bar’s name. Moderators are also notified of new reports. Nothing is sent about people you have blocked or who have blocked you. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings, under Espresso Martini, Notifications.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase: the database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and Apple Maps for the map, bar search and the bar suggestion.
  • Google: sign-in, only if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • GitHub: keeps the daily backups (in a private repository) and hosts these pages.

These services may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, as part of running their service. We do not use them to track anyone.

Magnus, as the operator, can see everything that is stored, in order to run and fix the app. Moderators see reports and the ratings and profiles they concern.

How long data is kept

  • Your data is kept while you have an account.
  • You can edit or delete any of your ratings at any time; deleting a rating deletes its photos. You can change or remove your profile photo; the old file is deleted.
  • When a moderator removes a rating, it is hidden from everyone and its photo files are deleted, though copies remain in backups for up to 90 days. The rest of the rating stays stored, hidden, so a mistaken removal can be undone.
  • Unfollowing someone or taking back a cheers removes it from view straight away. The notification record, and the note that it already notified (who followed or cheered whom), are kept until either account is deleted; for a cheers, also until the rating is deleted.
  • Backups: the database and photos are backed up once a day, and each backup is kept for up to 90 days. Backups are private. They do not contain device tokens or sign-in sessions, but they do contain everything else, including the email addresses of accounts that use Sign in with Google. A copy downloaded to check that a backup can be restored is deleted after the check.

Children

The app is not for anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.
