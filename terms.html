Espresso Martini is a free iPhone app for rating espresso martinis at bars and sharing those ratings. It is made and run by Magnus Stjernström, an individual. “We” and “us” on this page mean him and the people he asks to help moderate the app.

By using the app you agree to these terms. Please also read the Privacy Policy, which says what the app stores and who can see it.

In short: you must be 21 or older. Everything you post is public. Rating photos show the drink, not people. Nothing objectionable. Anything can be reported, and we act on reports.

Who can use the app

  • You must be of legal drinking age, which is 21 in the United States. The app is not for anyone younger.
  • The app is offered in the United States.
  • You are responsible for what happens on your account. Signing in uses your Apple or Google account; keep it secure.

What the app is, and is not

  • The app does not sell alcohol and does not take orders or payments.
  • Ratings, prices and comments are the opinions of the people who posted them, not ours. Bar names, addresses and menu details can be wrong or out of date.
  • Please drink responsibly, and never drink and drive.

Everything you post is public

Your username, your profile photo, your ratings (the bar, stars, price, tags, comment, photos and when you posted), your ranked list, and who you follow and who follows you can be seen by anyone who uses the app, including people who are not signed in. The Privacy Policy lists the few other things, such as your internal account number, that are in the app’s public data without being shown in the app.

Blocking someone stops contact inside the app: while signed in, they no longer see your ratings, photos or profile, and they cannot follow you or cheers your ratings. They are not notified, but they may be able to work out that you blocked them, because your content stops appearing for them. Blocking does not stop viewing: because everything is public, a signed-out visitor can still see your public ratings.

What you may post

  • Rating photos show the drink, not people. A rating photo that shows an identifiable person who has not agreed to it can be reported and will be removed.
  • Your profile photo may show you. It is public, like everything else.
  • Rate espresso martinis you actually had, honestly.
  • No objectionable content. That includes harassment or bullying, hate, threats, sexual content, violence, anything illegal, spam or advertising, pretending to be someone else, other people’s personal information, and anything you do not have the right to share. It also includes encouraging drinking by anyone under 21 or dangerous drinking.
  • The same rules apply to usernames.

Reports and moderation

  • Anyone signed in can report a rating or a person from the app. A rating you report is hidden from you straight away.
  • We review every report, normally within 24 hours.
  • We may remove a rating (its photo files are then deleted, though copies remain in backups for up to 90 days), remove a profile photo, or suspend an account, without warning. The app does not notify the person whose rating or photo was removed; a suspended person sees a notice in the app.
  • A suspended account cannot post, follow or cheers. Its ratings, photos and profile are no longer shown to others in the app, and its ratings, rating photos and profile cannot be read by others from the app’s public data either. Its photo files are not deleted unless we remove the rating, so a photo still opens for anyone who already has its web address. Others may be able to tell that an account is suspended. The person can still sign in, look around, and delete their account, and can write to us about it.
  • We do not tell anyone who reported them.

Your content

What you post stays yours. By posting it, you allow us to store it and show it to anyone in the app. If you delete your account, you allow us to keep showing the parts of your ratings that the Privacy Policy lists (stars, price, tags, time and drink photos), without your name, as “Former member”.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open My list, tap your photo (or initial) at the top left, then tap Delete account. The Privacy Policy says exactly what is removed and what is kept.
