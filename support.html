Support

Questions, problems, or something you want removed? Write to magnus.stjernstrom@gmail.com.

Report a rating

On a bar’s page or in the Feed, tap the … button on the rating, then Report. Choose a reason: Shows a person, Not an espresso martini, Offensive, Spam, or Something else (with an optional note). The rating is hidden from you straight away, and a moderator reviews the report, normally within 24 hours.

Report a person

Open their page by tapping their name or photo, tap the … button at the top, then Report.

Block someone

Open their page, tap the … button at the top, then Block. You stop seeing each other’s ratings, photos and profiles in the app, and they can no longer follow you or cheers your ratings. They are not notified, but they may be able to work out that you blocked them, because your ratings and profile stop appearing for them. Blocking stops contact, not viewing: everything posted is public, so a signed-out visitor can still see your public ratings.

To unblock, open My list, tap your photo (or initial) at the top left, and tap Unblock next to their name.

Edit or delete a rating

In My list, tap the bar. Each of your visits there has Edit and Delete. Deleting a rating also deletes its photos.

Delete your account

Open My list, tap your photo (or initial) at the top left, then tap Delete account. The app says exactly what is removed and what is kept before anything happens, and you confirm once more. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm by signing in once more. The Privacy Policy lists what is removed and kept.

Notifications

The app asks once, after your first follow or your first rating. To change your answer later, open iOS Settings, then Martini, then Notifications.

Location

Location is optional. Without it the map, search and feed still work; you choose the bar yourself. To change it, open iOS Settings, then Martini, then Location.

A suspended account

If your account is suspended, the app says so on the rating screen and on your own page. You can still look around and delete your account. To ask about it, write to magnus.stjernstrom@gmail.com.

Contact

For questions, reports about content, or requests about your data, write to magnus.stjernstrom@gmail.com.
